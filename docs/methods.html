Methods

How field productivity is measured from satellite greenness, how weather is corrected for, and how the suggested decisions are derived.

The methods text is being written. The methods agent is writing it as methods/METHODS.md in the repository, and this page will hold the same text once it is settled.

What the page will cover

  • The greenness record: Sentinel-2 surface reflectance with Cloud Score+ masking, composited on Earth Engine and read at 30 m.
  • The growing season per crop and state, taken from the CONAB planting and harvest calendars.
  • The weather adjustment: how precipitation (CHIRPS) and temperature (ERA5-Land) changes that move greenness without moving yield are removed.
  • Relative productivity: a field against the median of its peers in the same crop and region, in 2020 and in 2024.
  • The change classes and their thresholds, and the handling of fields whose crop class changed.
  • Pasture vigor from MapBiomas Collection 10.
  • The decision rules that map the evidence to restore, switch crop, change land use pattern, abandon, intensify, improve yields or keep.
  • What the measure can and cannot say.
