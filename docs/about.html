Map Methods

About this map

A public web map that shows, field by field across Brazil, where crop and pasture productivity rose or fell between 2020 and 2024, broken out by crop type.

Purpose

The map is meant to say, at the field level, which land use decision could be improved: restore the land, switch the crop, change the land use pattern, abandon the field, intensify, or improve yields on the same crop.

Productivity is measured from the greenness record over the growing season, with the growing season taken from CONAB crop calendars per crop and state. The measure corrects for precipitation and temperature changes that move greenness without moving yield. The quantity of interest is relative productivity: a field against its peers in the same crop and region, compared between 2020 and 2024.

Pasture fields additionally use the MapBiomas Collection 10 pasture vigor product, which classifies each pasture pixel as low, medium or high vigor, already normalized by biome.

The suggested decisions are screening results from satellite greenness. They are not agronomic advice. The method does not know prices, soils, tenure, inputs or the farmer's plans, and a field flagged here deserves a visit before any decision is taken on it.

Geography

The target is all of Brazil. If compute does not allow it, the first tier is Mato Grosso, Pará, Amazonas, Acre, Rondônia, Roraima and Amapá; the second is Mato Grosso do Sul, Goiás, Tocantins and Maranhão. The state selector on the map lists every state; states without tiles yet show nothing.

Code and data

The site, the pipeline and the methods text are in the repository at github.com/tristangrupp/relative-land-productivity. The field tiles are served as PMTiles from a public bucket; the file docs/DATA_CONTRACT.md lists every attribute the map reads.
